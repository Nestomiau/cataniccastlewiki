Catty Navio

Stats

  • HP - 3 Hearts(6 Hits)
  • Gravity - 5u
  • Jumpforce - 14u
  • Walkspeed - 6u

Moveset

Run Key (Shift)

When held, Catty will start running, doing a little jogging motion as he speeds up to 8u of movespeed, when reaching said speed, he'll be able to destroy blocks and damage enemies in his path, at the cost of sliding if trying to stop or turn around.

After reaching the 8u of movespeed, Catty's acceleration will be reduced substantially and his max speed will be set to 14u, going down slopes can accelerate him over the cap, but only to a max of 20u.

The slide can be used to actually turn around, losing some speed in the process.

Primary Attack (X)

Punch & Kick (Default)

If up is not pressed, Catty can do one of 2 actions, either a punch or a kick depending if he's grounded, both doing 2 of damage and setting their speed to 12u if they're slower. The attack can be cancelled by pressing the opposite direction of movement, but, if airborne, the attack cannot be cancelled until touching back on the ground or if the player was previously running.

Uppercut (Up)

When up is held before the attack, Catty will do a uppercut, dealing 2 damage and being sent flying by 150% of his Jumpforce and maintaining most his speed as long as you don't turn. The attack can is not cancelled until Catty touches a surface.

Secondary Attack (C)

Catty's secondary attacks are compromised of his usage on his mighty Cleaver(s), holding one in each hand, and are mainly focused on slashes and acrobatics, being great for maintaining momentum.

Slash (Default)

Catty will do quick swing of his cleaver, sending a slash projectile forwards, dealing 2 damage while also maintaining all control over himself. this can be extended into another slash by holding the key.

Cleaver Slam (Up/Down)

Catty will be sent upwards(converting horizontal speed into vertical speed if up was held) and will start falling rapidly as well as losing most of his horizontal speed, when connecting with the ground, a shockwave projectile will be sent in the direction he was moving, and he'll get stunned for .1 seconds, this hoever can be combo'ed.

Jump Combo(Z)

When combo'ed with a Jump, Catty will do a long jump out of the slam into a running state, getting a massive speed and height boost as well as converting earlier vertical speed into horizontal speed.

Primary Cancel(X)

When mid-air, this attack can be cancelled with a kick, resetting his horizontal momentum.

Secondary Cancel/Combo(C)

Catty will do a spin, converting vertical speed into horizontal speed and being fully controllable as long as the key is held, but being unable to jump/attack/run until the key is released, which will make him do a little bounce upwards before being able to run or attack again.

Wall Slide (Passive)

When running/using a primary attack against a wall while in the air or while on top of a ramp, Catty will start sliding it upwards, being able to exit it by releasing the run bind or by jumping out of the wall, keeping most of the movespeed.

Style-or-Debt (Passive)

Killing enemies with his secondary in quick succession gives a Style-bonus of 10% for each kill, resetting after 2 seconds without killing.
