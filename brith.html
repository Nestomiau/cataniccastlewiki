Brith Navio

Stats

  • HP - 2 Hearts(6 Hits) & 2 Junk Heart (4 Temporary Hits)
  • Gravity - 5u
  • Jumpforce - 14u
  • Walkspeed - 6u

Moveset

Run Key (Shift)

When held, Brith will start using his skateboard, immediately gaining 10u of movespeed and doing a little jump, he's always able to break blocks and damage enemies while skating and is very capable of turning around with his board, at the cost of a minor speed loss.

After reaching his max speed of 12u, Brith will only be able to accelerate by doing Tricks, or by going down slopes, but he'll lose that speed, quickly going back to his max speed.

Brith's Jumpforce is decreased by 35% while skating, this does not affect any height gained by Tricks.

Primary Attack (X)

Brith's primary attacks are dependant on if Brith's is using his skate, being the primary tool for movement or momentum gain.

Trick (Skateboard)

Whenever Brith leaves the ground or reaches the top of a slope, he has a grace period where he can peform a Trick, giving him a height boost of 150% his Jumpforce, setting him on a Trick State, and a speed boost of 5u once he lands on the floor.

While on a Trick State, Brith mantains the Skate controls.

Brith can only store 5 boosts(25u) from Tricks while in the air.

Jetpack (Default)

Brith will do a little spin before using his Jetpack, flying infinitely towards the direction pressed(or direction he was facing if none was pressed) at the speed of 12u, and after a couple seconds he'll start to fall into the ground, this attack can be cancelled with a jump, another Jetpack or a Trick

The time before he starts falling is not reset until he touches the ground while not using the Jetpack. He also can land directly onto the floor face first if going directly downwards, getting stunned for .2 seconds.

Secondary Attack (C)

Mic Toss

Brith will toss his Microphone, which will be a projectile attached to his body, doing 2 damage on contact and destroying all blocks, Brith can dodge the mic to keep it moving around him, the mic can't be tossed again until it's picked up.

That's Cash, In The Trash! (Passive)

While your time on the current level is under 1:00, Brith will receive a 25% Money multiplier.

Wall Bouncer (Passive)

Whenever Brith touches a wall while on his skate or jetpack, he'll bounce of the wall in a Trick state, getting the vertical height and once landing the speed boost.
