Floors

Floors are the main scenarios where you will traverse in-game, having a unique theme, enemies, perks and level desing. Floors will be compromised of 3 levels, sending you to a Elevator Shop after the third level is beaten.

In a run, there will be 8 floors(24 levels total), compromised of:

  • 2 Early-Game Floors
  • 4 Mid-Game Floors
  • 2 Late-Game Floors

Floor Concepts

Here is the list of Floors planned to be in the game, we plan on adding 16 Floors, here is the list of currently planned floors.

Early-Game Floors

Iearni Mines

The Iearni Mines is a dark and dim set of caves and tunnels, populated by the Moles and other "low-classed" beings, it's a mix between closed and horizontal tunnels and tall vertical pillars of rock.

This is a replacement to "The Mines", nothing will really change aside from ditching the green and blue colors.

Fwll Blwn Roads

Fwll Blwn Roads is a colorful city-like system, populated by Rivils, Flies, your average civilian. Having a bigger focus on vertical movement, having to go above the buildings or inside of them.

This is a replacement to "The Halls", being a complete redesing and concept change, going from the repetitive orange hall-like rooms to what it is "now".

Mid/Early Game Floors

Westside Train Station

A Western Styled Train Station, located at the west of the Castle and looking like a desert with a the sun setting in the background and making everything look darker. The population is mainly made of Cowboy-like Cats and civilians.
